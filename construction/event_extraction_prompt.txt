ROLE
Extract source-supported conditions relevant to scenario annotation.

INPUT
- Target and fixed domain background
- Forecast interval
- Relevant entity, geography or scope, and role or relation if applicable
- Dated source passages

TASK
Identify distinct events or conditions that:

1. are explicitly supported by the supplied sources,
2. overlap or remain relevant during the forecast interval, and
3. correspond to a mechanism in the fixed domain background.

For each condition, record:
- factual condition,
- timing and scope,
- event status (observed, expected, announced, alleged, resolved, etc.),
- applicable mechanism and required assumptions,
- conflicting evidence or unresolved information.

RULES
- Use only the supplied sources.
- Do not use outside knowledge.
- Do not infer events from the target trajectory, model forecasts, reference-relative direction, or calibrated departure score.
- Do not infer that a condition caused, dominated, or explains the target movement.
- Preserve uncertainty and distinguish occurrence from expectation, announcement, allegation, and resolution.
- Mark missing applicability conditions explicitly rather than filling them in.
- If no usable condition is supported, return INSUFFICIENT.

OUTPUT
evidence_status, extracted_events, unresolved_items
