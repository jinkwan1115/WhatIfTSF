ROLE
Audit a proposed factual scenario against its original evidence and the fixed domain specification.

INPUT
- Fixed domain background and mechanism map
- Proposed scenario and claim-to-source mapping
- Source-supported event records and original source passages
- Forecast interval
- Relevant entity, geography or scope, and role or relation if applicable

CHECK
For every substantive claim, verify:
1. direct support from the cited source,
2. correct timing, scope, entity, and event status,
3. applicability of the assigned mechanism and its required assumptions,
4. preservation of uncertainty and necessary qualifications,
5. fidelity after compression or de-identification,
6. absence of realized target information, forecasting instructions, or unsupported causal interpretation.

RULES
- Use only the supplied evidence and fixed domain specification.
- Do not use outside knowledge.
- Do not use or infer validity from the realized target trajectory, construction forecasts, reference-relative direction, or calibrated departure score.
- Do not repair unsupported claims with plausible assumptions.
- Any unsupported claim necessary for the scenario's interpretation requires HOLD_SAMPLE.
- A revised scenario must be reviewed again before acceptance.

OUTPUT
verdict: PASS or HOLD_SAMPLE
claim_verdicts, required_corrections, unresolved_items
