ROLE
Construct a fixed domain background for a scenario-conditioned forecasting benchmark.

INPUT
- Target definition and units
- A collection of reference documents, each with a source ID, title, URL, publication date, and one or more passages

TASK
Read the supplied documents together and synthesize the mechanisms that recur across the literature and are important for the target. Group equivalent mechanisms and distinguish general domain knowledge from document-specific examples. Do not produce one separate background per document.

Write:
1. a concise definition of the forecasting target, and
2. a compact set of general driver mechanisms that can affect it.

For each mechanism, describe:
- the relevant condition or driver,
- its conditional upward or downward pressure where supported,
- assumptions, applicability conditions, and limitations required for that interpretation.
- why it belongs among the domain's main mechanisms, including agreement, disagreement, or limited coverage across the supplied documents.

RULES
- Use only the supplied reference material.
- Include only general mechanisms that can be reused across benchmark episodes.
- Exclude benchmark episodes, anchor-specific entities or dates, dated forecasts, contemporary settings, and episode-specific expectations.
- Do not predict the target or assign numerical effect sizes.
- Do not infer that any mechanism actually occurs in a benchmark episode.
- Preserve uncertainty and conditionality; do not turn context-dependent relations into universal directional rules.
- Omit mechanisms not supported by the supplied sources.
- Judge prominence using target relevance, source authority, and consistency across documents; citation frequency alone does not establish importance or a universal effect.
- Treat multiple passages from the same document as one source of support. Do not mistake repeated or dependent accounts for independent confirmation.
- Keep the background compact. Preserve the source connections and unresolved issues in the mechanism map so that a person can review and revise the draft before it is fixed.
- This output is a draft for human review; do not mark it approved or proceed to candidate-specific scenario construction.

OUTPUT
background_text,
mechanism_map: mechanism, supported directions, required assumptions,
               limitations, supporting source IDs, selection rationale, unresolved_items
